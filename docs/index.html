About this map

Orientation, not ontology.

The Observatory turns the repository’s conference lists and citation index into an explorable research landscape. Papers are positioned by conference year and research category; detail cards distinguish formally published papers from officially accepted papers.

Connections are computed from shared metadata and title vocabulary. They suggest useful reading paths; they do not claim citation, influence, reproduction, or direct rebuttal.
